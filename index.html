The Mission

"Complex public data shouldn't require a policy degree to understand it."

I bridge the gap between foundational training in mathematics and computer science and national public policy, to make data literacy accessible to the next generation of Indian builders, not just those already inside the system. Dawai Sahi Hai, my entry for Vande Bharatam 2026, is this belief applied to a real problem: a medicine verification tool built because no consumer-facing check existed for India's counterfeit drug supply chain. The same instinct drives every data narrative I publish: take a number the public was never shown clearly, and make it impossible to misread.

Vande Bharatam 2026
Dawai Sahi Hai

A smartphone-first medicine verification tool. One in five medicine strips in India is counterfeit and the government's own QR system can be copied. This checks scan-volume anomalies and community reports instead, patterns a copied code can't fake.
